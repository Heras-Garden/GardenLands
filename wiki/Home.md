# GardenLands Wiki

GardenLands owns land, properties, housing, rentals, citizenship, mailboxes, access controls, territory presentation, and Garden chat formatting.

## Main systems

- Territory, city, district, property, building, and apartment claims
- Property addresses and sale signs
- Apartment and building mailboxes
- Rentals and hotel-style stays
- Chest, copper chest, barrel, and door access settings
- Copper golem container access
- Citizenship and territory flags
- BlueMap markers
- Garden chat formatting
